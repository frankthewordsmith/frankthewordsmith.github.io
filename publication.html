Publication rule

Uncertainty stays visible.

A passage verified online is not silently upgraded into a locked print/critical-edition citation. Reconstructed and fragmentary materials remain marked.

Reader's publication guide

1. Find a claim

Start with the timeline, synthesis, or research library.

2. Open the source

Follow the source ID to its record, locator, edition information, and evidence grade.

3. Check the evidence

Read what is secure, what is interpretive, and what remains unresolved.

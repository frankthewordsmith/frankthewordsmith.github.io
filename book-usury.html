The central question

What is so distinctive about usury?

The book asks why riba receives such severe and unusual treatment in Islamic scripture, and whether history can help us understand the economic, legal and moral problem being addressed.

The site distinguishes primary evidence from later interpretation so readers can inspect the trail of evidence for themselves.

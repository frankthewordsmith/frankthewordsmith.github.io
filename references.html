The bibliography is deliberately connected to the source-record system. Each final entry should identify the author, title, edition or translation, publication details and stable location of the material being cited.

Primary sources

Exact editions first.

Before a quotation is published, record the edition, translator where applicable, page or section, and the relevant original-language passage.

Secondary scholarship

Interpretation should be visible.

Scholarly works can be attached to individual source records so readers can distinguish the primary evidence from later historical interpretation.

RESEARCH METHOD

How does the research archive distinguish evidence from interpretation?

The archive separates source identity, exact reference, original terminology, translation, context, historical claim, interpretation, and unresolved uncertainty. Evidence grades describe the status of the evidence rather than the importance of a source.

SOURCES

Why are primary sources emphasized?

The site's research method starts with identifiable passages and source records so readers can trace a historical claim back to the underlying evidence. Later scholarship and interpretation can then be considered without presenting them as the original source.

ARTICLES

How do the articles relate to the research library?

The articles are publication-facing investigations built from the site's source registry. Where appropriate, an article can point readers back to the underlying research record rather than functioning as a detached list of references.

RESEARCH METHOD

Can a future book have its own research and Q&A material?

Yes. The site architecture is author-wide, while books can have their own research sets, article sections, publication editions, and future Q&A material. This keeps later books from being hard-coded into the Book One structure.

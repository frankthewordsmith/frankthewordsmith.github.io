Research & writing

FranktheWordsmith is a source-first research and writing project built around books, ideas, primary passages, and traceable evidence.

Primary text first

Major claims are tied to individual source records. Each record can carry original wording, translation, locator, edition information, context, and interpretation separately.

Evidence is graded

A means edition/page or equivalent stable lock; B means a verified primary passage with a remaining edition/page limitation; C marks reconstructed, composite, fragmentary, or later-witness evidence; D marks unresolved research material.

A necessary distinction

Online verification ≠ print-page verification

An accessible digital text can establish that a passage exists and can supply a stable structural locator. It does not, by itself, establish that a particular pagination belongs to a specified critical or print edition.

Publication ethics

Uncertainty is part of the record

The archive does not hide unresolved edition locks, fragmentary witnesses, reconstruction, or competing interpretations merely to make the narrative cleaner.

Citation principle. Readers should be able to identify and locate the source from the citation. The Library of Congress likewise recommends supplying core bibliographic elements and source location for digitized primary sources. Library of Congress citation guidance.

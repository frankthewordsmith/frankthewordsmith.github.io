METHOD

Evidence before certainty.

The publication separates documented facts from interpretation. Each record is anchored to a source and assigned an evidence grade according to the strength, directness, and traceability of the supporting material.

Dates and descriptions are kept as precise as the evidence permits. Where uncertainty remains, it is stated rather than silently converted into certainty.

A

Direct / strong

Primary or exceptionally well-attested evidence directly supports the record.

B

Well supported

Strong secondary evidence or multiple consistent sources support the record.

C

Qualified

The record is plausible and sourced, but important uncertainty remains.

D

Disputed / weak

Included for transparency where the evidence is contested or limited.

SOURCE CONTROL

Stable, inspectable references

Every published record is designed to point back to a stable source page or identifier. The source layer is kept separate from the narrative so readers can inspect the evidence without losing the chronology.

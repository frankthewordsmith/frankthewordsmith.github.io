Book One / Central Question
Why Only Usury Invokes Allah's War?

The Central Question

Why does the Qur'an employ the extraordinary expression of war in connection with riba? The book treats that as a question to investigate, not a conclusion to assume.

The wording

Qur'an 2:279 contains the phrase commonly translated as an announcement of war from Allah and His Messenger. Understanding what that statement means requires attention to the surrounding verses, the Arabic terminology, early interpretation and the historical institution of lending at interest.

The investigation

The project asks what riba meant in different historical settings, how societies regulated it, what rates were permitted or prohibited, and how Islamic jurists understood the Qur'anic prohibition.

Questions the research must separate

Text

What do the primary texts actually say?

History

How did lending practices and legal rules develop?

Interpretation

How did later scholars explain the texts and their scope?

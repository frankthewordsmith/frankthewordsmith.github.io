What this record is for

The verse tells believers not to consume ribā, described in the verse as multiplied many times, and connects the instruction with fear of God and success.

The verse predates the longer passage in 2:275–279 in the traditional chronological ordering used by Muslim scholarship, though dating and chronology are scholarly subjects in their own right.

What still needs to be separated

The phrase “multiplied many times” is an important part of the textual evidence and should not automatically be treated as a definition of ribā in all cases.

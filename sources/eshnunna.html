What this record is for

Law 18a explicitly records different interest calculations for silver and grain loans: 20% for silver and approximately 33% for grain.

The law collection is an Akkadian legal text from the early second millennium BCE. The precise dating and relationship of the surviving manuscripts require specialist discussion; the CDLI record provides the composite text and transliteration.

What still needs to be separated

Eshnunna is particularly valuable for the research project because it gives an explicit, surviving numerical comparison between silver and grain lending before Hammurabi. This makes it a useful control against claims that Hammurabi invented the 20%/33% pattern.

What this record is for

The Bavli expands the Mishnah's rules through argument, examples and distinctions concerning loans, pre-arranged benefits, business structures and indirect interest.

The Gemara records layered discussions by named rabbis and anonymous redactors; it is not a single contemporaneous code.

Indian Bank Rates, 1891: Calcutta 4% and Bombay 5%

Date: 4 August 1891

Evidence grade: A

Primary source

House of Commons, Hansard, 4 August 1891, vol. 356, East India financial discussion.

Official Hansard record

Exact primary passage

“the rate of discount was very low, the bank rate never rising above 4 in Calcutta and 5 in Bombay, as compared to 12 in the two preceding years.”

What the passage establishes

This establishes the stated maximum bank rates for Calcutta and Bombay during the period discussed and the parliamentary comparison with the preceding two years.

Evidence limits

It does not identify individual banks, explain the full causal mechanism, or establish a universal market rate across India.

Historical significance

This is a distinct banking-market layer: official parliamentary evidence of a sharp change in regional bank-rate conditions.

What this record is for

The hadith describes categories of exchange in which unequal or deferred exchange of specified commodities constitutes ribā.

The report is central to the later doctrine commonly termed ribā al-fadl and ribā al-nasiʾa.

What still needs to be separated

This record concerns exchange of specified commodities and should not be silently equated with every later doctrine about monetary loans.

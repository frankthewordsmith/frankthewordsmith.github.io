India Fixed Rupee Exchange Rate, 1891

Date: 5 May 1891

Evidence grade: A

Primary source

House of Commons, Hansard, 5 May 1891, vol. 353, cols. 132–133, “Rupee Rate of Exchange”.

Official Hansard record

Exact primary passage

“a rate is fixed for all transactions for the ensuing year, irrespective of the fluctuations of the market. The fixed rate for 1891–2 is 1s. 6½d.”

What the passage establishes

The parliamentary record states that a rate was fixed for all transactions for the ensuing year irrespective of market fluctuations, and gives the fixed rate for 1891–92 as 1s. 6½d. per rupee.

Evidence limits

This establishes the stated annual fixed-rate rule and the 1891–92 rate. It does not by itself establish every administrative procedure, every class of transaction in practice, the resulting fiscal gain or loss, or the broader rationale for the policy.

Historical significance

This is a distinct institutional-finance layer: it documents annual fixed exchange-rate administration for Indian Government transactions rather than merely reporting a market exchange rate.

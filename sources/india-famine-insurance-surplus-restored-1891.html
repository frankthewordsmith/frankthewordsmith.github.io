India Famine Insurance Surplus Restored, 1891

Date: 4 August 1891

Evidence grade: A

Primary source

House of Commons, Hansard, 4 August 1891, vol. 356, East India financial discussion.

Official Hansard record

Exact primary passage

“the surplus of Rx.1,500,000 for famine insurance was restored in its fulness.”

What the passage establishes

This establishes that the Indian Government restored the full stated annual famine-insurance surplus of Rx.1.5 million in the financial year discussed.

Evidence limits

It does not establish that a legally segregated fund existed; later parliamentary explanations distinguish the policy from a separate statutory fund.

Historical significance

This is an institutional fiscal-reserve layer: annual surplus revenue was deliberately restored to the famine-insurance provision.
